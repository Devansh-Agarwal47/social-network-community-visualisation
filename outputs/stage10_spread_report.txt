B) FAKE NEWS: a rumour starts at a random user (p = 0.12). Average users reached if we block k users first:
   blocked k     random users      top degree     top bridges
   0                     81.1            81.1            81.1
   5                     81.4            28.6            48.1
   10                    77.7             8.2            24.3
   20                    67.4             2.6             2.6
   40                    51.1             2.1             2.5
   -> blocking the 40 top degree users cuts the rumour from 81 to 2 users; blocking 40 random users only gets it to 51.
   Why hubs matter: with no blocking, 68% of rumours stay under 15 users, but the 31% that reach 100+ users
   cause 98% of all spreading. Those big outbreaks are the ones that get through a hub; removing hubs removes them.
